About me

13 years of running a back office from the Philippines

For over a decade, I was the remote backbone of a Canadian point-of-sale and equipment leasing operation. No local team, no manager checking in daily — just me, the work, and the responsibility to keep two companies running clean.

That meant everything from chasing AR and reconciling accounts to managing executive correspondence, coordinating vendors, and maintaining ISO-compliant records — all in QuickBooks Online, across time zones, without dropping the ball.

Before that: international debt collections in Dubai, operations coordination for KFC Philippines, and government administrative work. Every environment taught me something different. The throughline was always the same — show up, take ownership, deliver.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Accountancy from the University of San Agustin, Iloilo City. I'm based in Rizal, Philippines and available immediately for full-time remote roles.
