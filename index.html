About me

13 years running a cross-border back office

For over a decade, I was the remote backbone of Condor Solutions Ltd. — a Canadian point-of-sale and equipment leasing operation — supporting both Canadian and US-side business simultaneously, from the Philippines, without missing a beat.

That meant owning the full customer journey — from signed quote to completed installation — alongside executive correspondence, vendor coordination, ISO-compliant records, CAFT payment setup, and supporting a sister leasing company running concurrently. All of it, remotely, across CAD and USD, from the Philippines.

Before that: international debt collections in Dubai, operations coordination for KFC Philippines, and government administrative work. Every environment was different. The throughline was always the same — show up, take ownership, deliver.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Accountancy from the University of San Agustin, Iloilo City. Based in Rizal, Philippines. Available immediately for full-time remote roles.
